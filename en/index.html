Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Purpose of the service

The “Sunory” application (the “Application”) works out the sun's course for the place where the user stands: sun position, sunrise and sunset, twilight, golden hour and blue hour. The information it provides is for guidance only.

2. Accuracy and safety

The Application's calculations rest on published astronomical formulas, cited in the Application (“Calculations and sources” screen). They are estimates: terrain, buildings, weather and altitude are not taken into account, and precision drops near the poles. The publisher cannot be held liable for decisions made on the basis of this information (photography, outdoor activities, navigation, etc.).

Never look directly at the sun, nor through the camera, the Application's 3D view, binoculars or any optical instrument: it can cause serious and permanent eye damage.

3. Privacy and personal data (GDPR)

We take the privacy of your data extremely seriously:

  • No collection: the Application asks for no sign-up, no name and no email address.
  • Location: if you allow it, the Application reads your phone's approximate location, only while you use it (never in the background), and only to work out the sun's course. An approximate location is enough: on Android, the Application only asks for approximate location. The calculations run on your phone. Your location is neither stored nor sent to the publisher or third parties: it is read again each time the Application opens. You can withdraw access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Camera: if you allow it, the 3D view shows the rear camera image to lay the sun's course over it, only while that screen is open. The image is only displayed: it is neither recorded, nor analysed, nor sent to the publisher or third parties. The Application does not use the microphone. You can withdraw access at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Phone location service: the location comes from your phone's system (Apple on iPhone, Google on most Android phones), under its own privacy rules; the Application adds no data to it.
  • Local storage: only your settings (the Application's language, onboarding already seen) are saved, on your phone only. Like the rest of your apps' data, they may be included in backups of your phone that you make yourself (for example iCloud).
  • No sharing: the Application sends no data to the publisher or to third parties. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no error reporting.
  • External links: the “Calculations and sources” screen opens third-party pages (scientific publications) in your phone's built-in browser. Those sites have their own privacy rules; the Application sends them no data.
  • Deletion: deleting the Application from your phone permanently and immediately deletes all of its data.
