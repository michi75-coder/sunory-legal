How are sunrise, sunset and the golden hour worked out?

With NOAA's formulas, based on Jean Meeus's astronomical algorithms, checked against NREL's reference algorithm. Sunrise and sunset are when the top of the disc touches the horizon, refraction included. The golden hour runs from +6° to −4° of sun elevation, then the blue hour down to −6°. Each calculation is explained with its sources in the app: Settings, then "Calculations and sources".

Why does the time differ from another app?

Golden hour definitions vary between sources by a degree or two. And no app sees your real horizon: a mountain, buildings or the weather can hide the sun before the calculated time.

Why does Sunory ask for my location?

The sun's course depends on where you stand. An approximate location is enough: a few kilometres move the sun by hundredths of a degree. Your location is only read while you use the app, is neither stored nor sent, and access can be withdrawn in your phone's Settings.

Near the poles

During the midnight sun or the polar night there is no sunrise or sunset that day: the app says so. Beyond 72° of latitude, the calculations lose precision.

My data
